Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

HeartConfia (“we”, “us”) is a place to share relationship stories anonymously. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain language on purpose.

The short version. Other people only ever see your username, never your email. We don’t sell your information. We don’t show ads. You can ask us to delete your account and its content at any time.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password (stored securely by our sign-in provider, never readable by us), or your Google account’s email if you choose “Continue with Google”. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Your username: a random name is created for you. It is the only identity other people see. If you change it, your content shows the new name.
  • What you post: confessions, comments, replies, verdicts (votes), reactions, saved stories, follows and reports you make.
  • Activity details: things like your streak, reputation level, achievements, and when you were last active, so features such as streaks and levels work.
  • Notification settings and devices: your alert preferences and, if you turn on phone alerts, the technical address your browser or phone gives us to send them.
  • Heart Credits: your credit balance and a record of every credit you receive or spend.
  • Payments: when payments are available, they are handled by our payment provider (Flutterwave). We receive confirmation of the payment and its reference, not your card number or bank login.
  • Private messages: if you use private messages, we store your message requests, chats and blocks. Chats are visible only to the two people in them. If a chat is reported, our moderators can read its recent messages. Messages are stored as ordinary text in our database. They are not end-to-end encrypted, so people with access to the database could technically read them. We limit that access, and our moderators read only chats that have been reported. Messages are deleted automatically after about a year (365 days), and when you delete your account. Notes and chats cannot contain links, email addresses or phone numbers, and we refuse messages containing words on a safety list.
  • Gifts, rewards and invites: we record gifts you send or receive (the sender is not shown to the receiver), daily rewards you collect, achievements, and who invited you or whom you invited (shown to you only as numbers).
  • Technical data: our hosting and database providers may log things like your IP address and device type for security and to keep the service running.

How we use it

  • To run HeartConfia: show your posts under your username, count verdicts and reactions, send the alerts you asked for.
  • To keep people safe: review reports, remove harmful content, and restrict accounts that break our rules.
  • To email you: a welcome message, password reset links, and, unless you turn them off in your profile, story replies, a weekly digest and occasional “we miss you” notes. Every such email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To keep credits and payments accurate and prevent fraud.

We do not use your stories for advertising and we do not sell or rent your information.

Who else handles your data

We use trusted providers to run the service, and they process data on our behalf: Supabase (database and sign-in), Netlify (website hosting), Resend (sending email), Google (only if you choose Google sign-in), and Flutterwave (payments, when available). They may store data outside Nigeria. We only share what is needed for each purpose, and we may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect someone’s safety.

Anonymity

Other members cannot see your email or your account details. Please help protect yourself: don’t post names, phone numbers, addresses or other details that could identify you or someone else. Our team can see account details when we need to run or protect the service, and we limit who has that access.

Drafts and polls

Drafts you save are private to you, are not read by other users, and are deleted when you delete them, when you publish them, or when you delete your account. Poll votes are stored with your account so you can only vote once, but other people only ever see totals, never who voted for what.

Collections

Collections are private until you choose to make them public. A public collection shows your anonymous username, the collection name and note, and the stories in it. Who follows a collection is not shown to anyone, only the number of followers. Collections you delete, or that belong to an account you delete, are removed.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and content while your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your profile and the content linked to it. Some records, such as the credit and payment history, may be kept for as long as the law or our accounting duties require, without your personal details attached where possible. Backups are cleared on a regular schedule.

Your choices and rights

Under Nigerian data protection law (the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023) you can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a purpose such as marketing email. You can turn off emails and alerts in your profile at any time. To use any of these rights, email hello@heartconfia.site from the email on your account. We aim to reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Age

HeartConfia is for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we will remove it.

Security

We protect your information with access controls, encrypted connections and limited staff access. No service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong password that you don’t use elsewhere.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@heartconfia.site
