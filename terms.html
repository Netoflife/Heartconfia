When payments are available, they are processed by Flutterwave under its own terms. We never see or store your card details. If a payment is taken but credits don’t appear, contact us with your payment reference and we will fix it.

7. Private messages, gifts and invites

  • Message requests. You can ask the author of a story or comment to talk privately. A request costs Heart Credits, and the other person can accept or decline. Chats show usernames only. Notes and chats cannot contain links, emails or phone numbers, and messages with words on our safety list are refused. You can archive a chat or delete it for yourself. Chats are deleted automatically after about a year. If someone blocks or declines you, you cannot keep asking them. If a request is not accepted within 7 days, the credits are returned to you.
  • Behave kindly in private too. The community rules apply to private messages. Do not harass, threaten, pressure, scam, or ask for money or personal details. You can block or report anyone from the chat. When you report a chat, our team may read its recent messages to decide what to do, and may switch off messaging for that account. Where the law requires it, or where someone may be in danger, we may share a reported chat with the authorities.
  • Gifts. You can send a gift to thank someone. A gift is paid for with your credits, the receiver gets a slightly smaller amount of credits (the difference is our fee), and gifts are not refundable. The receiver sees that a gift arrived, not who sent it.
  • Boosts and flair. You can pay credits to highlight or pin your own story for a limited time, or to unlock a profile flair. We may remove a boost from content that breaks the rules, without a refund.
  • Rewards and invites. Daily rewards, achievements and invite bonuses are free credits we may change, pause or stop at any time. We may withhold or remove rewards gained by cheating, such as making fake accounts or inviting yourself.

Polls: you may add a poll to your own story when you publish it. Poll choices follow the same community rules as stories (no personal details, no contact information, no abuse) and may be removed with the story. A poll may cost Heart Credits if a price is shown before you publish.

Collections: you may group stories into collections. Names and notes follow the community rules (no personal details, no contact information, no abuse). Making a collection public may cost Heart Credits, shown before you confirm; the cost is not refunded if a moderator later hides or removes the collection for breaking the rules. People can report public collections and moderators may hide them.
